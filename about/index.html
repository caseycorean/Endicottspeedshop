About

Not what to do next — why it's the right move.

Endicott Speed Shop exists because most automotive content tells you what people did. Almost none of it tells you why the decision made sense, what it was trading off against, or how you would think through the same problem on a different car.

The thinking behind this platform started in the early 2000s with a concept called SuperCar X. The premise was simple: treat the car as a variable. Pick any platform. Apply the same engineering philosophy. The specific car was never the point — the framework was. That idea sat in the background for twenty years while the builds got more serious and the thinking got sharper.

ESS is where that led.

The current work centers on second-generation Firebirds. The green car is the proof of concept — a budget build with honest constraints, designed to show that the philosophy produces results against cars costing far more. The white car is the full expression of the idea: an architectural build developed from the ground up, every decision deliberate.

The tools, articles and guides here are built around the same question those cars ask every day: not what do I do next, but why is this the right move? That question has an engineering answer. Usually more than one. The goal of this platform is to help you find yours.

Measure. Test. Improve. Know why.

Why the site says "beta"

Endicott Speed Shop launched in November 2026 and is still being built. Tools are checked against hand calculations before they go up, but they are new, and articles will be corrected and expanded as the cars are measured and tested. If something looks wrong, please tell us — corrections are welcome.
