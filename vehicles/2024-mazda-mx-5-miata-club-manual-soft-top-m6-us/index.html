2024 Mazda MX-5 Miata Club · manual soft-top

Club, standard brakes and wheels · 2-door soft-top roadster · 6-speed manual · US

The 2024 Mazda MX-5 Miata Club · manual soft-top is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 181 hp and 151 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 2,368 lb curb weight with 52% on the front axle — about 13.1 lb per horsepower.
