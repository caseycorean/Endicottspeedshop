2005 Aston Martin DB9

Early DB9 V12 coupe with automatic. Later DB9 power revisions and Volante are excluded.

The 2005 Aston Martin DB9 is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 450 hp and 420 lb-ft, with a automatic. Published figures give 3,880 lb curb weight — about 8.6 lb per horsepower.
