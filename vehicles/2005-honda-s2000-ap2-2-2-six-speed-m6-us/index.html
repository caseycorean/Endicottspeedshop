2005 Honda S2000 AP2 · 2.2 six-speed

AP2 2.2 · 2-door roadster · 6-speed manual · US

The 2005 Honda S2000 AP2 · 2.2 six-speed is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 240 hp and 162 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 2,835 lb curb weight with 49% on the front axle — about 11.8 lb per horsepower.
