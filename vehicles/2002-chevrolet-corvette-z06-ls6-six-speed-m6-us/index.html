2002 Chevrolet Corvette Z06 · LS6 six-speed

Z06 · 2-door fixed-roof coupe · 6-speed manual · US

The 2002 Chevrolet Corvette Z06 · LS6 six-speed is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 405 hp and 400 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 3,118 lb curb weight with 53% on the front axle — about 7.7 lb per horsepower.
