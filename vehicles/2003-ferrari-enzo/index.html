2003 Ferrari Enzo

Enzo road car. The test publication’s horsepower and test weight use its published units and conditions.

The 2003 Ferrari Enzo is a mid-engine, rear-drive car rated at 650 hp and 485 lb-ft, with a 6-speed automated manual. The cited road test weighed 3,262 lb as tested weight — about 5.0 lb per horsepower.
