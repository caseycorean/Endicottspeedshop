2002 Pagani Zonda S 7.3

Zonda S 7.3 factory specification. Published dry weight is kept separate from a curb or test weight.

The 2002 Pagani Zonda S 7.3 is a mid-engine, rear-drive car rated at 555 hp and 553 lb-ft, with a six-speed manual. The manufacturer lists 2,822 lb dry weight — about 5.1 lb per horsepower.
