1994 McLaren F1

1994 McLaren F1 road car as reported in the period road test. Keep the LM and GTR separate.

The 1994 McLaren F1 is a mid-engine, rear-drive car rated at 618 hp and 479 lb-ft, with a 6-speed manual. The cited road test weighed 2,579 lb as tested weight — about 4.2 lb per horsepower.
