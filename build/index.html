The build

The green car: a 1977 Firebird Formula, measured.

Status updated November 2026

The green car is the proof of concept: a budget LS1/T5 swap with honest constraints. The engine is a 2004 GTO LS1; the transmission is a T5 from an '84 Z28. The patina stays — if the car performs against cars costing far more, the patina becomes the point.

Where it stands

  • Drivetrain installed with clutch and flywheel; driveshaft in; headers in.
  • Standalone engine harness and controller mounted; battery relocated to the trunk.
  • C5 Corvette front brakes; fuel and brake plumbing largely done.
  • Next: finish wiring and final assembly, then first start.

What gets measured

Every part going on or off the car is weighed. The whole car is weighed on four corner scales at each milestone, and the scale differences give each major part's weight and location. The finished car will be weighed with fuel, fluids and driver recorded, and taken to autocross.

Latest recorded weighing (July 2026, not yet complete — no nose, hood, radiator, exhaust, fuel or coolant): 2,786 lb, 51% front.

What comes next

The green car is the baseline. Later development explores moving the engine rearward, Corvette front suspension, a three-link rear axle and, eventually, a reworked structure and underbody aerodynamics — each step measured against this car.
