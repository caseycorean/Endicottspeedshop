What Second-Gen Firebird Parts Actually Weigh (Measured)

Endicott Speed Shop · Measured March–July 2026

Published part weights for second-gen F-bodies are scattered, rarely say what was included, and often disagree. These are numbers we measured ourselves while stripping one car and building another — with what each one includes.

How these were measured

Two methods, and they have different strengths:

  • Component scale. Parts small enough to lift were weighed on their own (a bathroom or food scale; the exact scale wasn't always recorded, so treat these as good to roughly a pound, not a tenth).
  • Corner-scale difference. For big assemblies, the car sat on four corner scales; we removed the part and weighed again. The difference is the part's weight, and how the difference splits between the front and rear scales tells you where the part sat. You can do the same with our scale-difference tool.
Read the "includes" column. Most disagreements between published part weights come from different things being included, not from bad scales.

Whole car

A stripped shell (no engine, transmission or front subframe) weighed 1,646 lb on our scales. The green car, built back up with the LS1/T5 but still missing its nose, hood, radiator, exhaust, fuel and coolant, weighed 2,786 lb at 51% front in July 2026. The finished-car weighing comes next.
