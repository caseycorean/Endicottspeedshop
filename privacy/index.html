Privacy

Privacy

Last updated November 2026

Endicott Speed Shop is a small, independent site. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising, and it does not sell personal information. The only outside service it uses is Google Analytics, which receives the site-statistics information described below.

What is collected

Site statistics. This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and tools are used. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and collects information such as the pages you view, how you arrived (for example from a search or a link), your general location (country or region), device and browser type, and time on the page. We see these as totals and trends, not as named individuals.

Calculator use. When you use a calculator, the site records that a tool was started and that it produced a result, with the tool's name. The numbers you type into a calculator are not sent anywhere; every calculation runs in your own browser.

Email. If you email the shop, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and use it only to reply.

Why

To learn which articles and tools are useful, fix what isn't, and decide what to build next.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site and all tools work the same without cookies. Google's own practices are described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Links to other sites

Articles may link to other websites. Their privacy practices are their own. If this site ever earns a commission from a link, that will be stated next to the link.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If what the site collects changes, this page will be updated and its date changed.
