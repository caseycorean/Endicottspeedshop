    How it works. Weight moves to the front as the car slows (W·h·a/L). Each tire loses a little friction as its load rises (the same 15% load sensitivity the oval uses), so a lower CG helps slightly. ABS keeps each axle near its peak (95% of it, estimate). Without ABS the fixed brake split decides which axle reaches its limit first. Brakes build over 0.15 s (estimate). Air drag helps at speed. Not modelled: downforce, brake fade over repeated stops, road surface changes.

    Validation. Run against all 24 published stops in the ESS vehicle records without tuning: Car and Driver stops average 5% longer in the model (mean miss 7%). Tire class matters most: cars on track-oriented tires (911 GT3) stop much shorter than their default tire class suggests.
